Central Post Office

The history of the Eritrean post office is marked by periods of external control and eventual independence. Initially, Egyptian post offices operated in Massawa from 1869, later followed by Italian post offices when Eritrea became an Italian colony in 1890. Following British occupation during World War II, British stamps overprinted for Eritrea were used. The Ethiopian Post took over postal sovereignty in 1952 after the forced federation with Ethiopia, which lasted until Eritrea's independence in 1993. Today, the Eritrean Postal Service (EPS) operates independently with over 50 post offices.
